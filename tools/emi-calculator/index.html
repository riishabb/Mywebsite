EMI Calculator Nepal

Reducing-balance instalment calculation with a reconciled amortisation schedule.

Calculation method

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1), where P is principal, r is the periodic interest rate and n is the number of payments. The final instalment is adjusted for rounding.

How to use this calculator

Use the calculator to estimate instalments for home, vehicle, personal or business loans. Enter the principal, annual interest rate, tenure, tenure unit and payment frequency.

Important limitations

The calculation assumes a constant interest rate and regular payments. Bank fees, insurance, taxes, prepayment charges, changing rates and irregular repayment dates are not included.
