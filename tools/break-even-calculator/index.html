Break-Even Point Calculator

Estimate the sales volume and revenue required to cover costs or reach a target profit.

How to use this calculator

The tool divides fixed costs plus target profit by contribution per unit, which is selling price less variable cost per unit.

Important limitations

The estimate assumes constant selling price, variable cost and sales mix. Step costs, capacity limits and taxes are excluded.

Free browser-based calculation for preliminary analysis. Review contractual terms and obtain appropriate professional advice before making a financial decision.
