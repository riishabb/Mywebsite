NFRS 9 Effective Interest Rate Calculator

Solve the periodic and annual effective interest rate from the initial carrying amount and contractual cash flows.

Calculation method

The calculator numerically solves for the rate that discounts contractual coupon and maturity cash flows to the initial carrying amount. It then applies that rate to each opening balance to produce an amortised-cost schedule.

Practical example

For a loan issued below its face value, enter the face value, net initial carrying amount after eligible fees, coupon rate, payment frequency, term and maturity cash flow. The difference between cash interest and EIR interest adjusts the carrying amount.

Documents and assumptions to review

Contract, disbursement and repayment schedule, transaction-fee analysis, premium or discount, expected cash flows where applicable, modification history and accounting classification.

Important limitations

This calculator assumes a regular coupon pattern and one maturity cash flow. It does not determine classification, expected credit losses, floating-rate resets, modifications, prepayments or which fees form part of the EIR.
