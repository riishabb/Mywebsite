Provision Assessment

Consider whether a past event has created a responsibility that may require payment.

Use this assessment as a documented starting point

Answer one plain-language question at a time, review the explanation where needed, and save the final conclusion together with the contracts, approvals and other evidence used. The result highlights areas requiring judgment; it does not replace a complete review of the applicable NFRS or NAS requirements.
