NPV and IRR Calculator

Evaluate an initial investment and periodic net cash flows.

How to use this calculator

Enter the initial investment and later cash flows in chronological order. NPV discounts them using your required rate; IRR estimates the rate at which NPV is zero.

Important limitations

IRR may be unavailable or misleading for unconventional cash-flow patterns, and this tool assumes equally spaced periods.
