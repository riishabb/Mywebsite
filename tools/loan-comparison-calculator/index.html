Loan Comparison Calculator Nepal

Compare instalments, fees and total cost for two loan offers.

How to use this calculator

Use identical principal and tenure inputs to compare two interest rates and their upfront fees on a consistent basis.

Important limitations

The comparison assumes fixed rates and regular monthly instalments. It excludes insurance, taxes, penalties and rate changes.

Free browser-based calculation for preliminary analysis. Review contractual terms and obtain appropriate professional advice before making a financial decision.
