NAS 33 · Nepal Financial Reporting Standards

NAS 33 Earnings per Share Calculator

Calculate basic EPS and test an aggregate potential-share adjustment for dilution.

Calculation method

Basic EPS is adjusted earnings attributable to ordinary shareholders divided by weighted-average ordinary shares. The indicative diluted calculation adds entered potential shares and related incremental earnings only when the result is dilutive.

Practical example

Start with profit attributable to ordinary shareholders, deduct preference dividends, and enter weighted-average ordinary shares. Add potential ordinary shares and related post-tax earnings adjustments to review whether they reduce EPS.

Documents and assumptions to review

Statement of profit or loss, preference-share terms, daily or monthly share movement record, bonus and rights issue terms, options, warrants and convertible-instrument calculations.

Important limitations

A complete NAS 33 calculation normally requires transaction dates, retrospective bonus adjustments, rights issue factors and instrument-by-instrument dilution tests. This simplified tool uses aggregate inputs.
