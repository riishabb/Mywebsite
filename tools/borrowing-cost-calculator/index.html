NAS 23 Borrowing Cost Calculator

Prepare an indicative calculation of specific and general borrowing costs for a qualifying asset.

Calculation method

Specific borrowing cost is based on the specific borrowing, rate and capitalisation period after entered investment income. General borrowing cost applies the entered capitalisation rate to the weighted general-borrowing balance, subject to the expenditure cap.

Practical example

For a construction project financed partly by a project loan and partly from general borrowings, calculate specific interest first, deduct temporary investment income, then add the general borrowing amount attributable to qualifying expenditure.

Documents and assumptions to review

Loan agreements, interest statements, qualifying-asset expenditure ledger, construction timeline, suspension periods, investment income and weighted general-borrowing calculation.

Important limitations

The tool does not determine whether an asset qualifies, establish commencement or suspension dates, calculate a weighted expenditure base, or apply limits across multiple general borrowings automatically.
