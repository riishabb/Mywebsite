Depreciation Calculator

Compare straight-line and reducing-balance depreciation schedules.

How to use this calculator

Straight-line depreciation spreads depreciable amount evenly over useful life. Reducing-balance depreciation applies the entered rate to the opening carrying amount without reducing it below residual value.

Important limitations

Accounting depreciation requires management estimates and applicable reporting policies; tax depreciation may follow different rules.
