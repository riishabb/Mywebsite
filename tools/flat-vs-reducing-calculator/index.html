Flat vs Reducing Interest Rate Calculator

See how the same quoted annual rate can produce different borrowing costs.

How to use this calculator

A flat rate applies interest to the original principal throughout the term, while a reducing rate applies interest to the outstanding balance. The same quoted percentage can therefore produce very different costs.

Important limitations

Confirm how the lender calculates instalments and includes fees before relying on the comparison.
