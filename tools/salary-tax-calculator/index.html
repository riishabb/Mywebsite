Rates, sources and scope

FY 2083/84 (2026/27) only. Rates and relief checked on 17 October 2026 against the Inland Revenue Department’s published natural-person tax table. Calculations run on your device; salary inputs are not uploaded or saved.

The first NPR 1,000,000 is taxed at 1% unless an eligible exemption applies; the next NPR 500,000 at 10%; the next NPR 1,000,000 at 20%; the next NPR 1,500,000 at 27%; and income above NPR 4,000,000 at 29%. Each rate applies only to the income in that band. Combined approved retirement contributions are deductible up to the lowest of actual contributions, NPR 500,000 or one-third of assessable employment income.
