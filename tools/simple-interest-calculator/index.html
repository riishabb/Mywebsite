Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate interest where interest is not added back to the principal.

How to use this calculator

Simple interest applies the entered annual rate only to the original principal. It is useful for basic short-term estimates where interest does not earn further interest.

Important limitations

Confirm whether the quoted rate, day-count basis, taxes and fees match your actual contract.
