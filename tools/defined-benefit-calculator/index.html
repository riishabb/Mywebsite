NAS 19 · Nepal Financial Reporting Standards

NAS 19 Defined Benefit Obligation Estimate

Create a preliminary estimate of a retirement benefit before obtaining a professional actuarial valuation.

This preliminary estimate is not a substitute for a professional actuarial valuation.

Calculation method

The simplified working projects salary and service to retirement, applies the entered benefit formula, adjusts for a constant attrition assumption and discounts the projected benefit to the present.

Practical example

Enter current salary, age, retirement age, completed service, annual salary growth, discount rate, attrition rate and benefit months per year of service. Review the projected salary, projected benefit and discounted estimate.

Documents and assumptions to review

Employee census, plan rules, salary history, retirement age, actuarial assumptions, government-bond yield support, past employee turnover and prior valuation results.

Important limitations

This is not a projected-unit-credit actuarial valuation. It omits mortality, age-specific withdrawal, demographic assumptions, plan assets, service-cost allocation, remeasurements and benefit probability by employee.
