NAS 19 Defined Benefit Obligation Calculator

Indicative simplified projected-unit-credit calculation using user-entered assumptions.

This tool provides an indicative calculation for educational and analytical purposes and is not a substitute for a professional actuarial valuation.

Calculation method

The simplified working projects salary and service to retirement, applies the configurable benefit formula, adjusts for entered attrition and discounts the result. Mortality and other actuarial assumptions are not invented or inferred.
