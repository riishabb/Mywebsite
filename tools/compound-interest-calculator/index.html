Compound Interest Calculator

Project growth when earned interest is added to the balance.

How to use this calculator

Compounding adds earned interest to the balance, so later periods earn returns on both principal and accumulated interest.

Important limitations

The result assumes a constant rate and no deposits, withdrawals, taxes, fees or timing differences.

Free browser-based calculation for preliminary analysis. Review contractual terms and obtain appropriate professional advice before making a financial decision.
