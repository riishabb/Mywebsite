Financial Ratio Calculator

Calculate common liquidity, leverage and profitability ratios from entered figures.

How to use this calculator

The calculator provides current ratio, quick ratio, net profit margin, return on assets and debt-to-equity using the figures entered.

Important limitations

Ratios should be compared across periods and with suitable peers. Classification, averaging and industry differences can materially affect interpretation.

Free browser-based calculation for preliminary analysis. Review contractual terms and obtain appropriate professional advice before making a financial decision.
