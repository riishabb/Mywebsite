NFRS 16 · Nepal Financial Reporting Standards

NFRS 16 Lease Calculator Nepal

Estimate the initial lease liability, right-of-use asset and payment schedule without building a spreadsheet from scratch.

Calculation method

The calculator discounts unpaid lease payments using the selected payment frequency and timing. The right-of-use asset starts with the calculated liability and adjusts for entered prepayments, direct costs, restoration obligations and lease incentives.

Practical example

For a five-year lease with monthly payments, enter the annual discount rate, 5 years, monthly frequency and whether payments occur at the beginning or end of each period. Review the opening liability, finance cost, principal reduction and closing balance for every month.

Documents and assumptions to review

Lease agreement, payment schedule, commencement date, renewal or termination options, incremental borrowing rate support, incentives, initial direct costs and restoration estimates.

Important limitations

The result is indicative. It does not decide whether a contract contains a lease, determine the enforceable lease term, select the discount rate, account for modifications, taxes, foreign exchange or reassessment events.
