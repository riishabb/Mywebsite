NFRS 16 Lease Calculator

Indicative lease liability, ROU asset and finance-cost working using present-value mathematics.

Calculation method

Lease liability is the present value of unpaid lease payments, with payment timing reflected in the annuity factor. ROU asset reconciliation includes user-entered prepayments, direct costs, restoration obligations, incentives, residual guarantee and purchase option.
