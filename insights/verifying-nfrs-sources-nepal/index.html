Where to verify NFRS requirements in Nepal

A source hierarchy helps prevent an old summary, training slide or search result from being treated as the current requirement.

By Rishab Dahal · Published and last reviewed 24 September 2026

Scope: General educational information. Confirm the standard and its effective status for the reporting entity and period.

Start with the standard setter

The Accounting Standards Board Nepal states that it is responsible for formulating accounting standards for the preparation and presentation of financial statements in Nepal. Its publication pages are therefore the first place to check the text or availability of an NFRS edition. As of this article's review date, the Board's website includes a publication page for NFRS 2024.

Then verify pronouncement and effective date

A publication's existence does not by itself answer every applicability question. Check ICAN's NFRS resources and effective-date material for the pronouncement relevant to the reporting period. Also check sector-specific legislation or regulator instructions where they affect the reporting framework.

Use a four-step source hierarchy

  1. Applicable law and regulator requirement: identify which framework the entity must use.
  2. ASB Nepal standard text: read the relevant recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure requirements.
  3. ICAN pronouncement and effective-date information: confirm the version and reporting periods covered.
  4. Secondary commentary: use articles, model statements and examples to understand application, not to replace the primary requirement.

Record what you relied on

A short technical memo should identify the reporting period, entity category, standard edition, relevant paragraphs, facts considered, judgments made and conclusion. Save or reference the source and access date when an online page may change.
